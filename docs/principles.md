# 🧭 Principles

These are the five principles that guide every decision in the **BUSUP Design System** — from foundational tokens to complex flows. They help us build products that are coherent, accessible, and a pleasure to use.

---

## 🌐 Adaptive

Our Design System is built to be **adaptive**.

The experiences we create must work on any screen, platform and language, ensuring that all users can access and interact with our products efficiently.

---

## 🔁 Consistent

**Consistency** is key to reducing the mental burden on users.

By keeping positions, styles and behaviors predictable, we ensure that users receive exactly what they expect, making it easier to transfer knowledge to new contexts and allowing them to focus on executing tasks rather than learning how the product interface works.

---

## 💡 Intuitive & Clear

Our interfaces should be **intuitive and clear**, using familiar patterns to make experiences easy to understand and recognize.

We take simplicity into consideration, always focusing on hierarchy, proximity, alignment, contrast, negative space and repetition in the interface.

---

## 🔬 Considerate

Design decisions should be based on **industry standards, best practices and research data**, not opinions or compromises.

We dive deep into problems and work with a clear purpose. Although perfection is unattainable, we always seek to improve and deliver better products for our users.

---

## 🚀 Empowering

Our components and interfaces are designed to fully **empower users** to complete their tasks.

We are goal-oriented, optimizing for the most important tasks, but always giving people access to more details if necessary.
